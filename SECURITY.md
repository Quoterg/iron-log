# Security policy

Iron Log is local-first: your data stays on your device. If you find a vulnerability, please report
it privately through GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting**
(repository → Security → "Report a vulnerability") rather than a public issue.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the app version. We aim to answer within a week.
See `docs/SECURITY-REVIEW.md` for the known findings and decisions.
